# Sources are kept in their official language

EU Sources are indexed in English and German Sources in German, as published. The Answer Model answers in the language of the question and quotes German provisions in German, with a paraphrase when answering in English. The alternative, indexing unofficial English translations of German acts, was rejected: translations lag behind amendments and are not what a supervisor cites. The cost is that retrieval must work across languages, which the multilingual embedding model handles. Decided in the planning session of 2026-10-20/21.
